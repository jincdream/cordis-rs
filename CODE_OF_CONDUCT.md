# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone. Be constructive,
assume good intent, focus criticism on ideas and code, and respect differing experience and
backgrounds. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not
accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the security/contact channel
listed by the repository owner. Reports will be handled confidentially and without retaliation.
